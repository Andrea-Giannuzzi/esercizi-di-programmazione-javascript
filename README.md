# Free Programming Exercises
Here you will find a series of computer science exercises divided by topic and difficulty (easy, medium, and hard). These exercises are completely free, whether you are one of my students in the [Corso di Informatica: Programmatore Perfetto](https://www.programmaconalberto.it/p/corso-di-programmazione-programmatore-perfetto) or you are not taking my lessons and simply want to practice. I decided to publish them for free to give everyone the opportunity to get plenty of practice, since **the best way to learn programming is through practice**.

Enjoy the exercises and... happy coding!
